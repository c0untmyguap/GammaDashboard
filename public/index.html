Educational tool. The bot learns from simulated price paths whose behavior is an assumption about how dealer gamma affects price, from level behavior in real sessions you've recorded, and from your scored paper trades. Confluence counts and entry triggers are rules, not predictions. Results are not predictions and not investment advice. Data: CBOE delayed quotes (~15 min) and Yahoo Finance candles.

Proxy backtest · last month

This is not a test of the gamma thesis. It tests the confluence rules, strike selection and exits with a 20-day-average stand-in where the gamma levels should be. A real gamma backtest will replace it once the server has archived a few weeks of actual option chains (checking archive…).
How to read this: trades are replayed on real 15-minute SPY bars, real VWAP / 2-day AVWAP, realized vol and VIX history. Historical options open interest isn't available for free, so past gamma levels use a stand-in: regime from price vs its 20-day average plus VIX vs its 20-day average, flip = 20-day average, P1/walls = round $5 strikes around the expected move. Option prices are modeled (Black-Scholes, VIX-scaled IV, today's skew shape), not real fills. The forward test at the bottom uses the real gamma levels.
Window: trades that expired during the last 21 trading sessions. Entries at 10:00 ET; intraday = 0DTE, weekly = 5 sessions, monthly = 21 sessions.
